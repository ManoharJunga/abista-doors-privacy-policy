# Privacy Policy – Abista Doors App

**Effective Date:** 07 January 2026

Abista Doors (“we”, “our”, “us”) operates the Abista Doors mobile application (the “App”).

## Information We Collect

### Camera
Used to capture site photos during sales visits. Photos are captured only when the user takes them.

### Location
Foreground location is collected to record sales visits and track employee attendance during working hours only.

### User Information
Name and phone number for authentication and internal business operations.

## How We Use Information
- Sales visit reporting
- Employee attendance tracking
- Internal workflow management

## Data Sharing
We do not sell or share personal data with third parties.

## Data Security
All data is transmitted securely and stored using protected servers.

## User Control
Users can disable camera or location permissions from device settings.

## Children’s Privacy
This app is not intended for children under 13.
